DegreeAdmin

  • A web-based system designed to simplify academic planning for both students and academic advisors.
  • The platform provides a centralized interface where users can manage, review, and adjust academic progress in a structured and efficient way.

What the System Does

  • Allows students to select and manage multiple majors and minors within a single system
  • Automatically generates required courses, prerequisites, and dependencies based on selections
  • Builds structured semester-by-semester schedules to guide students toward graduation
  • Provides role-based access so students, faculty, and administrators interact with tailored interfaces

Key Benefits

  • Reduces confusion surrounding complex degree requirements and academic pathways
  • Helps students plan more efficiently and minimize delays in graduation timelines
  • Improves decision-making through clearer visibility into long-term academic goals
  • Supports faculty by streamlining advising and approval processes for course plans

What’s The Problem?

  • Students often struggle to plan complex academic requirements while balancing multiple majors and minors
  • Prerequisites, limited course availability, and scheduling conflicts make planning difficult and confusing
  • It can be unclear whether a student is on track to graduate within their intended timeframe
  • Academic advising is frequently a manual process that can be time-consuming and inconsistent

Why This Matters

  • Poor academic planning can lead to increased costs and delayed graduation
  • Students often lack visibility into long-term requirements and dependencies
  • Faculty and advisors spend significant time reviewing and correcting student schedules
  • Without structured tools, planning becomes inefficient and prone to error

Our Motivations

  • Create a system that simplifies and streamlines the academic planning process
  • Help students make informed, confident decisions about their academic paths
  • Provide a structured and automated way to evaluate graduation progress and requirements
  • Improve the overall experience for both students and faculty through better tools and organization
